Practice

Labs

Work through these in order. Each lab lives under labs/ in the repo — open a terminal next to this page.

01

First repo

Init, stage, commit — your first real history.

 02

Branch & merge

Split work safely, then bring it back together.

 03

Conflict

Meet a merge conflict and resolve it calmly.

 04

Rebase

Replay commits and keep history readable.

 05

Undo

Restore, amend, reset, and revert with intent.

 06

Remote & PR

Push a branch and open a clean pull request.

 07

Team workflow

Ignore junk, sync often, write reviewable PRs.

 08

Stash

Shelf unfinished work, switch context, restore it.

 09

Tags

Annotate releases and check out a version tag.

 10

Cherry-pick

Bring one commit onto another branch.

 11

Interactive rebase

Squash messy commits into a clear story.

 12

Bisect

Binary-search history for the breaking commit.

 13

Internals

Walk commits, trees, blobs, and refs.
